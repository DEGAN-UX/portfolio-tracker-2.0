    2. Results

    What "total return" includes: price gain (today's value + sale proceeds − purchase cost) plus dividends and fund distributions (after withholding tax), minus trading fees. Purchases and income are converted to CHF at the rate DEGIRO actually used; today's value uses today's ECB rate. "Annualised" is the money-weighted yearly return (XIRR); it is not shown for holdings bought less than a year ago.
